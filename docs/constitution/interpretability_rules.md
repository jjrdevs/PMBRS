## Interpretability Philosophy

PMBRS requires partial interpretability.

Internal models may be complex.

However, all user-facing conclusions must remain traceable to:

- observable artifacts,
- reproducible computations,
- statistically supported associations.

Opaque unsupported assertions are prohibited.

## Interpretability Constraints

User-facing conclusions must:

- reference observable artifacts,
- expose transformation lineage,
- expose uncertainty,
- distinguish correlation from causation,
- identify modality limitations where relevant.

## LLM Role Constraints

LLMs function strictly as:

- interpretive assistants,
- summarization systems,
- hypothesis-generation tools,
- semantic labeling systems.

LLMs are not authoritative sources of truth.

LLM outputs:

- remain probabilistic,
- remain reproducible,
- remain replaceable,
- must preserve provenance.

## Forbidden Interpretive Behaviors

PMBRS must not:

- claim psychological truth,
- diagnose conditions,
- fabricate causality,
- overwrite observational evidence,
- recursively redefine authoritative artifacts.
