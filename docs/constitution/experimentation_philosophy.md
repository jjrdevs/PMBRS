## Experimentation Philosophy

Structured self-experimentation is a primary purpose of PMBRS.

Experiments allow behavioral comparisons between:

- interventions,
- routines,
- schedules,
- environments,
- habits,
- contextual changes.

## Experiment Definition

An experiment is a temporally scoped declaration representing:

- an intervention,
- a behavioral modification,
- a contextual change,
- or a comparison hypothesis.

Experiments are declarative artifacts rather than inferred artifacts.

## Experiment Constraints

Experiments must:

- preserve temporal boundaries,
- preserve lineage,
- preserve uncertainty,
- expose confound metadata,
- remain reproducible.

## Experimental Interpretation Constraints

PMBRS supports:

- statistical comparison,
- behavioral association analysis,
- longitudinal evaluation.

PMBRS does not autonomously claim:

- definitive causality,
- psychological truth,
- universal behavioral laws.

## Experiment Lifecycle

Experiments may contain:

- declaration,
- active phase,
- observation period,
- evaluation,
- retrospective comparison.

Experiments remain immutable historical records.
