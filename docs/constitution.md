### 1. Purpose & Identity

PMBRS ("Personal Behavioral Measurement, Experimentation, Representation System") is a **personal behavioral measurement, experimentation, and representation learning platform** designed to:
- Integrate multimodal longitudinal data streams  
  - Discover reproducible statistical associations between behaviors, interventions, contexts, outcomes using structured self-experimentation methodology 
  - Support evidence-informed personal interpretation.
